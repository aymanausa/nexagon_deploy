# Nexagon — Bridging the National Localization Gaps

How Nexagon contributes to four Vision-2030 localization objectives. Nexagon is the **intelligence
and decision layer** — it does not itself grow food or build factories, but it **directs, times, and
de-risks** the decisions that move each of these needles, and quantifies the outcome. Each gap below
is mapped to the concrete platform capabilities that address it, the mechanism, honest scope, and an
illustrative outcome.

---

## Gap 1 — Higher local content ratio

**The gap:** raise the share of food (and of the tech/data stack behind food security) that is
locally produced rather than imported.

**How Nexagon bridges it:**
- **Self-Sufficiency Radar (FAO FBS):** quantifies KSA production ÷ domestic supply per commodity vs
  a policy target and the GCC average — the baseline that makes "local content" *measurable* and
  shows exactly where the ratio is weakest (e.g. wheat/rice heavily import-reliant).
- **Water-aware localization:** the Food-Security Index's **Water** sub-indicator (KSA ~75 m³/capita —
  extreme scarcity) prevents the classic mistake of localizing water-intensive staples. It steers
  local-content growth toward **water-appropriate, high-value production** (greenhouse/controlled-
  environment produce, poultry) and keeps water-heavy grains as strategic imports + reserves.
- **Demand Forecast → local production signal:** the 12-month demand projection tells local producers
  and MEWA *how much* and *when* — the demand certainty that underpins investment in local capacity.
- **Auto-generated Initiatives:** the system proposes concrete, ownable "expand/secure local X"
  initiatives (linked to the pillar they improve) and tracks them to completion.
- **Local content of the platform itself:** Nexagon is a **Saudi-built software + AI asset** — the
  data/AI capability is local content, reducing reliance on foreign analytics platforms.

**Mechanism:** measure the current ratio → identify where local substitution is *feasible and
water-smart* → quantify the demand that justifies investment → track the initiatives that raise the ratio.

**Honest scope:** Nexagon directs and measures localization; the physical production is executed by
MEWA/growers/investors. Its role is to make those bets **evidence-based and water-responsible.**

**Illustrative outcome:** a dashboard where the self-sufficiency ratio per commodity is tracked over
time against a target, with initiatives that visibly move it — turning "local content" from a slogan
into a monitored KPI.

## Gap 2 — Spend retained in the Kingdom

**The gap:** keep more of the national food budget circulating inside KSA rather than leaking to
inefficient imports, poor timing, and avoidable premiums.

**How Nexagon bridges it:**
- **Procurement Optimizer (buy/hold/defer):** times purchases to price troughs (OLS forecast), draws
  on reserves instead of buying spikes, and flags forward-buying — directly **reducing import spend**
  on the same volume. Every recommendation shows its reasoning and an estimated quantity.
- **Risk-adjusted origin ranking:** ranks suppliers by *landed* desirability (implied price + political
  + climate + food-safety risk), avoiding costly disruptions and over-concentration premiums.
- **Reserve optimization (Stock Levels):** days-of-supply vs configurable thresholds → buy-and-store in
  cheap windows, release in expensive ones — time-arbitrage that retains value domestically.
- **Redirecting saved spend inward:** savings and demand certainty from the above are the fiscal room
  to fund **local production and reserves** (Gap 1/3) — spend redirected from imports to Kingdom capacity.
- **Software spend retained:** a locally-built platform replaces foreign SaaS analytics licences.

**Mechanism:** buy smarter (timing + origin + reserves) → cut import leakage → recycle the savings and
demand certainty into local capacity.

**Honest scope:** landed cost currently uses a Comtrade implied-price + risk-premium proxy (live
freight is on the roadmap); savings are directional until authoritative price/freight feeds land.

**Illustrative outcome:** "shifting X% of wheat from a high-risk origin to a diversified low-risk one,
and buying in the post-harvest trough, avoids an estimated $Y premium" — a repeatable, auditable saving.

## Gap 3 — Stronger local industry

**The gap:** build a more capable, resilient domestic food and agri-industry (and the data/AI industry
that supports it).

**How Nexagon bridges it:**
- **Demand certainty for producers:** the seasonal demand engine (Ramadan/Hajj/population) gives local
  processors and growers a credible forward demand curve — the single biggest enabler of private
  investment in local capacity.
- **Supplier diversification intelligence:** the concentration/HHI analysis and auto-initiatives push
  toward a broader, more resilient sourcing base — and identify where a **local** supplier could
  displace a fragile foreign one.
- **Food-safety & quality lens (RASFF/SFDA-ready):** benchmarking origin food-safety performance raises
  the bar and creates a quality standard local industry can compete on.
- **National capability building:** a governed, documented data platform (SDAIA-aligned) with reviewable
  assumptions creates durable **local data + AI capability** — institutional strength, not just a tool.
- **Whole-of-government coordination:** initiatives carry an owning authority (SAGO/SALIC/MEWA/SFDA),
  making the platform a shared operating picture that strengthens institutional coordination.

**Mechanism:** de-risk local investment with demand certainty + quality benchmarks + coordination →
stronger, more resilient domestic industry.

**Honest scope:** Nexagon enables and coordinates; industrial growth is delivered by the ecosystem.
Its contribution is the **shared, evidence-based operating picture** that industry and government act on.

## Gap 4 — Faster and better sourcing

**The gap:** make procurement decisions quicker, smarter, and more risk-aware — the gap Nexagon
addresses most directly and completely.

**How Nexagon bridges it:**
- **One decision surface:** Supplier Map + per-commodity origin breakdown + risk/climate/disease/food-
  safety overlays → *where* to buy, risk-adjusted, at a glance instead of across siloed reports.
- **Procurement Optimizer:** *what* to do now per commodity (buy/hold/defer), *how much*, *when*, and
  *from where* — with transparent reasoning. Turns weeks of analysis into an instant, auditable call.
- **News AI signals (GDELT + Claude):** the **leading indicator** — export bans, droughts, port
  disruptions surface days before trade/price data move, so sourcing reacts early, not late.
- **Scenario Simulator (incl. autonomous + natural-language):** test "what if this origin/route fails?"
  in seconds and get the mitigation + alternate origins — faster contingency planning.
- **Autonomous alerts + initiatives:** the system pushes "act now" (with a ready mitigation) the moment
  a material threat emerges, compressing detection-to-decision from weeks to same-day.

**Mechanism:** consolidate the signals → recommend the risk-adjusted action → surface it proactively →
let procurement act in hours, not weeks.

**Honest scope:** this is decision *support* — a human procures; Nexagon makes the decision faster,
better-informed, and defensible.

**Illustrative outcome:** an emerging Red Sea disruption auto-generates a corridor scenario, an alert,
and a "reroute via Jeddah + accelerate forward buy for wheat/rice" initiative — same day, with the
quantified index impact — versus discovering the exposure weeks later.
