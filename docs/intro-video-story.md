# Nexagon — Introduction Video Story

A story/script for a ~90–120 second introduction video. Includes the narrative arc, a scene-by-scene
shot list with voiceover (VO) and on-screen text, plus a shorter 30-second cut.

---

## The narrative in one line
A single headline on the other side of the world can threaten what's on Saudi tables — Nexagon sees
it coming, measures the impact, and turns it into action before it arrives.

## Tone
Calm, confident, national-scale. Not alarmist — *in control*. Deep navy + gold (the product palette),
clean data-motion, a human anchor (a decision-maker) so it's about people, not just dashboards.

---

## Scene-by-scene (≈100s)

**Scene 1 — The stakes (0:00–0:12)**
*Visual:* Aerial of a Saudi port at dawn; cranes unloading grain; a busy supermarket; a family meal.
Text fades in: **"80% of our food crosses an ocean to get here."**
*VO:* "Saudi Arabia feeds itself from the whole world. That strength is also a dependency."

**Scene 2 — The invisible risk (0:12–0:25)**
*Visual:* A world map; faint shipping lanes through the Red Sea and the Gulf. A distant news ticker
flickers — "export ban… drought… strait tensions." The lanes pulse red, one by one.
*VO:* "A drought in Brazil. An export ban in Asia. A closed strait. Each one moves toward our shelves —
usually, we find out too late."

**Scene 3 — Enter Nexagon (0:25–0:38)**
*Visual:* The map resolves into the Nexagon interface — the gold hexagon mark, then the
**Food-Security Index** dial settling on a number.
Text: **"Nexagon — National Food-Security Intelligence."**
*VO:* "Nexagon changes that. It gives the Kingdom one living measure of its food security — and the
intelligence to protect it."

**Scene 4 — It watches the world (0:38–0:52)**
*Visual:* News headlines stream in; each is tagged live — *commodity, country, severity*. A daily
brief writes itself on screen.
*VO:* "It reads the world's news as it breaks, and understands what each event means for Saudi supply —
days before it shows up in prices."

**Scene 5 — It stress-tests the future (0:52–1:08)**
*Visual:* Someone types in plain language: *"What if the Red Sea closes?"* The map reroutes around
Africa; the index dips from 71 to 70; commodities flip to **BUY**; a mitigation appears:
*"Reroute via Jeddah · pre-position wheat & rice."*
*VO:* "Ask it any 'what if' — or let it ask itself. Every day, Nexagon stress-tests emerging threats
and shows their impact before they land."

**Scene 6 — It turns insight into action (1:08–1:22)**
*Visual:* A recommendation card: *"Wheat — BUY now, pre-position ahead of the Ramadan peak."* It
becomes a tracked initiative with an owner (SAGO / SALIC) and a status moving to *In progress*.
*VO:* "And it doesn't stop at insight. Every signal becomes a clear, owned action — buy, diversify,
build reserves — tracked until it's done."

**Scene 7 — The close (1:22–1:38)**
*Visual:* Pull back to the full dashboard, calm and green; then the family meal from Scene 1, secure.
The hexagon mark centers.
Text: **"See it coming. Act in time. Nexagon."**
*VO:* "From a headline half a world away to a decision made in time. Nexagon — securing what matters most."

## 30-second cut

*Visual:* Port at dawn → red shipping lanes → Nexagon index dial → *"What if the Red Sea closes?"* →
map reroutes, index dips, BUY + mitigation → tracked initiative → secure family meal → hexagon mark.
*VO:* "Saudi Arabia imports most of its food — and the risks arrive before the data does. Nexagon reads
the world's signals, measures the impact on national food security, stress-tests every threat, and
turns it into action in time. See it coming. Act in time. **Nexagon.**"

## Production notes
- Real screens from the platform (index dial, supplier map with risk overlays, the scenario before→after
  panel, the news brief, an initiative card) make it authentic — use actual captured UI.
- Keep numbers on screen consistent with the demo (index ~71; Red Sea scenario 71→70).
- Music: restrained, building, resolving to calm — mirror the detect→act→secure arc.
